      Our Purpose

      MYGrocery was created as a university JavaScript Lab Assignment to demonstrate real-world web development skills. It helps Malaysian users plan their grocery shopping, calculate costs with sales tax and discounts, track monthly spending, and maintain a history of calculations for budget planning.

      The application uses 74+ real Malaysian grocery products with realistic prices in Malaysian Ringgit (RM), sourced from common retailers such as Jaya Grocer, Lotus's, AEON, Village Grocer, and Mydin.

      Malaysian Grocery Focus

      This website is designed specifically for Malaysian users. All prices are displayed in Malaysian Ringgit (RM) using the Intl.NumberFormat API. Products use metric units (kilograms, litres, pieces) and feature brands commonly found in Malaysian supermarkets. The product catalog includes items across Fruits, Vegetables, Meat & Seafood, Dairy & Eggs, Bakery, Drinks, Pantry, Snacks, and Household categories.

      Price Disclaimer: Product prices are representative Malaysian retail prices and may vary by store, location, promotions and date. Prices are based on common Malaysian grocery retailers such as Jaya Grocer, Lotus's, AEON, Village Grocer, and Mydin.

      Project Information

      MYGrocery is a university JavaScript Lab Assignment project. It demonstrates JavaScript captures grocery/shopping input, calculates total cost including sales tax, and displays the final amount dynamically. Error handling handles invalid input and missing fields. CSS enhances the website appearance and usability.

      This is a front-end only application. No real backend, payment processing, or online ordering is involved. User accounts and data are stored locally in the browser using localStorage.
